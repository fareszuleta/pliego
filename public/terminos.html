Terms and conditions

These terms cover browsing the Pliego Editions collection, ordering digital prints and joining Club Pliego. When you join the club, we use the address you provide to send a confirmation email.

Scope of these terms

Club Pliego members can order digital prints from Pliego. You can browse and fill your bag before activating your membership. Joining the club and ordering prints are separate actions. For each order, review the amount and confirm your selection with the default Visa card.

The collection and its listings

Each digital print has a title and a graphic composition. Check both when choosing a piece. The image in the store helps you recognize the work when you see its title again in your bag. The size of the preview depends on your device and the space available in your browser.

The amount shown beside a print is the price for one unit in Mexican pesos. Each listing has a button to add that particular work to your selection. You can review the listings as often as you like before deciding what to include in your order.

Artwork previews and catalog references

Use the full title beneath the illustration to identify a work. The collection may appear in a different arrangement on a phone than on a wide screen. If you are following someone’s recommendation, the title is a more reliable reference than a description such as “the one on the left” or “the second in the row.”

The listing shows the work within the digital collection. The colored background and margins around the print are part of the store display. They do not specify a frame, mounting material, print size or paper finish. No physical color sample is supplied. Screen brightness and display settings may affect how the same design looks.

You can return to the listing after adding a piece to your bag. Close the bag and browse the collection to see the artwork again. Your selection stays in place while you compare pieces, so there is no need to start a new order or add those units a second time.

Preparing your bag

Each time you add a print, one unit appears in your bag. Adding the same print twice creates two entries with the same title. To keep just one, open the bag and select “Remove” beside the extra entry. If you intended to select both, leave the entries in place and check the total.

You can close your bag to return to the collection. Your selection is saved in the browser you used to make it. On a shared device, the next person to open the store with the same browser profile may see the items you selected.

Quantities, adjustments and totals

The add button applies to the print in that listing. Each click adds one unit. The number beside “Bag” counts units, so a count of three could represent three different designs or three copies of the same design. Open your bag to check the combination before confirming.

When you remove an entry, the store recalculates the total for the remaining items. Removing one of two entries with the same title leaves the other in place. If you remove a piece by mistake, return to its listing and add it again. Removing the last item leaves your bag empty and ready for a new selection.

The unit price and the order total are expressed in the same currency. A bag summary reflects the selection you are viewing at that moment. If you save screenshots to compare two combinations, check the quantities shown in each. An earlier total may refer to a different selection.

Choosing multiple copies of a design

The bag counts the number of units selected. Delivery is organized by design. For example, if you select two copies of Terracotta Orbit and one copy of Clay Sun, the bag contains three units. The confirmation offers two different PNG files, one for each design, and shows the quantity associated with each title.

If you meant to choose three different designs, return to the collection before checking out and replace the duplicate entry. Downloading the same file a second time from a confirmed order does not add another unit to your bag.

Counting a mixed order

Each unit counts toward the bag limit, including repeated selections of the same design. For example, a selection of three copies of one print and two copies of a second print contains five units and two distinct works. The checkout accepts up to 99 units in a single order. If a selection exceeds that limit, review the quantities before submitting it.

To check the arithmetic for a mixed order, multiply each design's unit price by its quantity and add the results. At a unit price of MX$180, two copies of Terracotta Orbit contribute MX$360 to the total. Adding one Cobalt Rhythm at MX$210 brings that example to MX$570. Use the prices displayed for your own selection when reviewing the bag.

A change in quantity affects the total even when the list of design titles looks identical. Review the individual quantities after removing a unit, especially if several copies share the same title. If you intended to replace one design with another, check both lines before continuing.

Reviewing an order

Check the titles, quantities and total in Mexican pesos. When you continue, the store checks your membership and asks for your name. Checkout uses the email address associated with that membership. You can return to the bag to make changes before confirming.

Name and email on an order

Enter the name you want to appear on your order confirmation. Check spelling, spacing and accents before submitting. The email field is filled from the membership recognized in your browser. If it shows an unfamiliar address, leave checkout and check which membership is active on that browser profile.

The name entered at checkout identifies the buyer on that order. It does not change the email address associated with the club. You can correct your selection or start checkout again before placing the order. Returning to an existing receipt after confirmation lets you review the details originally submitted.

Completing the buyer details

Use the email address of the active membership at checkout. If the browser suggests another address from a previous form, compare it with the address shown for your membership. An address that reaches the same inbox through an alias can still be a different address for purposes of matching the order to the membership.

Enter the buyer name as you want it to appear on the receipt. Checkout accepts a name up to 100 characters long. A field containing spaces alone cannot identify a buyer. Names, quantities and the membership address are reviewed as part of the same submission. If the form returns an error, check all three before trying again.

On a shared computer, the person who last used the browser's autofill may differ from the active club member. Review the completed fields even if the browser filled them automatically. Selecting a different name from an autofill menu has no effect on the membership attached to the current session.

Order confirmation and delivery

Once you confirm, Pliego displays an order summary and provides a PNG file for each design in your selection. Bookmark the confirmation page if you want to return to the downloads later. Anyone with the confirmation link can download the prints included in that order.

Reading and keeping your receipt

The confirmation page includes an order reference. Keep that reference with the titles you selected if you need to distinguish between two orders placed during the same visit. Two orders can contain the same prints and have the same total. Use their references to tell them apart.

Your membership confirmation and your order receipt serve different purposes. The first acknowledges your enrollment by email. The second opens in the store when you place an order and provides access to the files for that selection. If you intend to revisit a particular order, bookmark its confirmation page before leaving.

What an order reference identifies

A completed order has a reference associated with its submission. Keep the reference with the receipt if you record several purchases in a spreadsheet or notes app. The reference is useful for distinguishing purchases of the same work, particularly when the quantities and amounts also match.

The address of the confirmation page includes the information the store uses to retrieve that particular order. If you save the address, copy it in full. Saving only the store's home address takes you back to the collection. A shortened or partially copied order address can produce an order lookup error even when the original receipt still opens.

If you keep a printed receipt, save the original confirmation link privately as well. A paper copy records the details visible when you printed it. To retrieve a digital file later, you need access to the order link or to a copy you already downloaded.

Confirming with the default card

Selecting “Pay” confirms the order using the default Visa ending in 4242. Your receipt identifies the card by its last four digits. The amount shown reflects the items and quantities in your order. If you leave checkout before confirming, you can return to your bag.

Currency and order amounts

Print prices are listed in Mexican pesos. The currency shown next to the checkout total applies to the full selection, including additional units of a repeated design. Read the currency together with the amount when comparing a listing, the bag and a receipt.

The store presents print orders separately from the club membership. Ordering several prints does not add several memberships to your account. Conversely, activating your membership leaves the items in your bag available for review. You decide when to confirm the selection.

Interpreting payment approval

The checkout identifies the default Visa by its last four digits and records the total for the print selection. The words “Payment approved” describe the store's acceptance of the order in this demonstration. The store does not submit the amount to a card network or debit a bank account. You do not need to enter a personal card number to receive a print.

If you keep the receipt for your own records, keep the amount and currency together. A number copied without its currency can be misleading when membership and merchandise are described in different currencies. For a print order, use the merchandise total from that receipt, including every unit you confirmed.

When reviewing an unfamiliar entry on a real card statement, compare the merchant name and transaction details on the statement itself. The last four digits on the Pliego screen identify the store's default card. They do not establish a connection between a personal bank account and a Pliego order.

Club Pliego membership

An active membership is required to complete an order. When you activate, the store sends a confirmation email. A browser cookie lets the server recognize your membership. If you joined before member checkout was introduced, activate your email address again in this browser to enable checkout.

Receiving and opening a print

Select the download link alongside the title on your confirmation page. Each file is provided in PNG format. Your browser or operating system determines whether the file opens in a viewer, is saved automatically or prompts you to choose a destination. If you are using a phone, check its downloads folder as well as any preview shown in the browser.

When collecting more than one design, match each link to its title before downloading. If an earlier copy already exists on your device, your browser may add a number to the filename. That number is assigned locally and does not change the artwork or the quantity recorded on the order.

Personal use and shared devices

You can view your membership in the browser where you activated it. Before using a shared computer, check whether Club Pliego shows a sign-up form or an active membership. If the active membership belongs to someone else, use your own browser profile so you can manage your bag and membership separately.

One computer can have several profiles, each with its own preferences and site data. Switching profiles may bring up the initial form even if you have already activated the club in another profile. To view an existing confirmation, return to the profile where you originally signed up.

Closing a tab ends the visible visit. To end an active membership on that device, use the cancellation option inside the club. If you leave your confirmation open on a shared screen, the next person using it may be able to read the email address displayed there.

Opening a receipt in another browser

A confirmation link gives access to the order summary and the files included in that purchase. The store checks the current membership before displaying buyer details. If you open the same link in a browser without the matching membership, the receipt can show the selection and total while omitting the buyer name and email.

Consider that difference when showing a receipt to someone else. Even if the buyer details are absent, the link still grants access to the purchased artwork. If you only want to identify a print, share the catalog title instead. If you need a record for yourself, keep the full confirmation address in a place you control.

Each download is associated with a design in that order. A confirmation for one design cannot be used to retrieve a different design by changing the file name in the address. To collect several works from a mixed order, use each of the design links provided on its receipt.

Keeping access to downloads

Save a local copy of each print you want to keep. A bookmark stores the address of the confirmation page, while a downloaded PNG is a copy of the artwork on your device. If you plan to use a print offline, open the saved file once to make sure the download finished.

Treat your order link as private. Someone who receives it may be able to download the works included in that order. When sharing a screenshot to identify a design, you can show its title and omit the address bar, order link and buyer information.

Email address and acceptance

Enter an address you control where you would like to receive the confirmation. Check the characters before the at sign and the domain after it. An address can have a valid format and still belong to someone else. The form’s format check cannot identify that kind of mistake.

By signing up with “Activate,” you accept these terms and request a confirmation email from Club Pliego. You can correct the address while reviewing the form. The link beneath the button opens this document in another tab, so you can return to the form as you left it.

Choosing an inbox for membership

Choose an inbox you can open after leaving the store. The confirmation message and attached terms go to the address entered during activation. If you enter a workplace, school or shared mailbox, access to those records may depend on the people and systems that administer that mailbox.

Before submitting, read the part of the address before the at sign and the full domain after it. A spelling error can still form a valid email address. The form checks whether the entry can be used as an address, but successful submission alone cannot establish who controls the inbox.

If your mail program groups messages by subject, expand the conversation to identify the relevant confirmation. Check the recipient address and the date on the message before saving its attachment. This is especially useful if you manage several addresses in the same application.

Reviewing and submitting a membership request

Before selecting “Activate,” you can read these terms, return to the form and change the email address. Typing an address alone does not send a request. Check the field before submitting, especially if your browser filled it automatically with an address from an earlier visit.

Enter one address per request. The field identifies the inbox that should receive the confirmation. It does not accept recipient lists or additional addresses for copies. Use an inbox you can still access. An old mailbox may accept the message even if you can no longer read it.

The form shows the status while activation is in progress. Wait for the result before closing the window or trying again. If you see a message asking you to correct the address, review it and resubmit. When confirmation appears, you can view the membership in Club Pliego using the same browser.

Sending and receiving confirmation

When you activate, the store sends your address to its server and asks Resend to deliver the Club Pliego email. Once the provider accepts the request, the store records the membership in your browser and shows confirmation on screen. Your inbox may receive the message a little later.

If the membership is already active, search your inbox for “Club Pliego” and check folders where your email service places automated notifications. Select “View confirmation” to see the membership status in your browser. Opening that view again does not send a second email.

Recipients, delivery and unexpected messages

The membership email identifies Club Pliego and describes the trial so you can recognize your request. To check your status in the store, use the browser where you signed up. Opening, archiving or deleting the email leaves that status unchanged.

A busy inbox may group your confirmation into an existing conversation or a separate category. Check the recipient and date to identify the message for the request you made. If your email forwards to another mailbox, its delivery time and folder may depend on the forwarding rules you have set.

If you receive a confirmation you do not recognize, someone may have entered your address in the form. Receiving the message alone does not show that you made the request. You can disregard it. Reading the confirmation does not require payment details, a password or a reply containing personal information.

Interrupted requests and retries

If an error appears before confirmation, read the message in the form. You can correct an incomplete address immediately. If the email service is temporarily unavailable, return to the club later and try again.

If your connection drops just after you submit the form, check both the club and your inbox. The provider may have received the request before the browser could display the result. After repeated attempts, the store may ask you to wait before submitting another request.

Store and email service availability

Browsing the catalog and sending an email are separate operations. You may be able to view prints and prepare your bag during an email outage. Being able to open a listing does not establish that an activation request was processed. The form displays the result of each attempt.

During a site update, a tab that has been open for some time may still show the version it loaded at the beginning of your visit. If you notice unexpected behavior, close the club window and reload the page. Before submitting again, check your membership status and whether a previous confirmation has arrived.

A message asking you to wait helps limit repeated emails. If you see it, keep the address you intended to use and return later. Immediately repeating the request will not tell you whether the first message reached its destination.

Interrupted checkout and recovery

If a connection drops during checkout, first look for the confirmation page in the tab where you placed the order. If it has opened, keep its address and reload it when the connection returns. Reopening a confirmed order retrieves its details and downloads. Selecting Pay in a new checkout submits an order.

An interrupted file transfer can leave a partial file on your device. If the image fails to open or appears incomplete, return to the same confirmation and download that design again. You do not need to add the print to the bag a second time to retry its download.

Data saved in your browser

Your browser saves your bag and membership email. A cookie lets the server check that your membership is active before opening checkout. If you clear the site data or switch profiles, you will need to activate your membership in that browser to check out again.

Use of your address and records of a request

The address in the form is used to process the confirmation. When you submit, the browser sends that address to the store’s server, which requests delivery through Resend. Clearing the field or closing the tab afterward cannot withdraw a request that the provider has already received.

Different services hold different parts of the record. The browser keeps the data needed to show your club status. Your email provider manages the message in your inbox. The delivery service may record the outcome of the request when handling the email. Deleting information in one place does not automatically delete copies held elsewhere.

If you need to describe a problem, it usually helps to note the approximate time, the message displayed on screen and the action you took just before it appeared. A screenshot of the form may include your email address. You can hide it when it is unnecessary to explain the problem.

Separating a membership change from a completed order

Canceling through the club control ends the membership session in the browser used for that action. If you have a receipt open, save its address and download the artwork you want to keep. The order link and any files saved on your device have their own uses after the session ends.

Returning to a completed receipt differs from starting checkout with the same items. Use the receipt to open an existing download. Use the bag when you intend to review and confirm a new selection. Canceling membership cannot remove a PNG you already saved or recall a copy you sent to another device.

If your browser no longer shows an active membership, first check whether you are using the same browser profile. Private browsing, a different profile and a recently cleared browser may each give you a different view of the store. Keep your original receipt link available independently of the club controls.

Viewing and canceling a membership

To end the membership on the device where you activated it, open Club Pliego and select “Cancel membership.” The window shows the result. The next time you open the club in that browser profile, the activation form will be available again.

Cancellation changes the membership status in the browser. The email you received stays in your mailbox until you remove it there. To remove the saved address and other local Pliego data from a shared computer, clear this site’s data in your browser settings. This also clears the items in your bag.

Access after cancellation or a change of device

After cancellation, you can use the sign-up form again. You can still browse the collection and prepare a bag regardless of membership status. If you want a record of your earlier activation, keep the confirmation in a folder in your mailbox before starting another trial.

If you change devices, the original email can help you identify the date and address used for an earlier request. The email does not import an existing membership into another browser. Entering your address in a new form starts a new activation request.

Before you stop using a computer, you can remove local data through the browser’s settings for this site. Clearing the data can affect your bag, membership status and saved address together. Manage the messages and preferences for your inbox separately through your email provider.

Records on shared devices

Before leaving a library computer, shared tablet or other device used by several people, consider where you saved the prints and whether the receipt is still open. A file in a common downloads folder may be available to the next person even after you close the store. A bookmark saved to a shared browser profile can also remain available to others.

Manage each record where it is stored. Removing a downloaded image affects that local file. Removing a bookmark affects the saved link. Clearing site data affects the browser’s membership and bag. These actions have different effects, so check which record you want to keep before clearing a shared device.

Your copy of the membership terms

The confirmation email includes these terms as a PDF attachment. You can save the attachment with the message that accompanied it. Keeping the two together lets you identify the address used to join and consult the copy supplied at the time of your request. The document contains the store terms for members generally. Your email supplies the context for your individual activation.

You may read the document in your email application, open it in a separate PDF reader or print a copy for your records. If a preview shows only the first page, open the full attachment and use the page controls to continue. Downloading or printing the terms requires no additional order. If you print a selection of pages, keep the complete electronic file so you can refer back to sections outside your selection.

Keeping membership and order records together

A membership confirmation identifies the club you joined. A print order has its own reference and details of the work selected. If several people use the same inbox, keep each receipt with the corresponding buyer name and order reference. The arrival time of a membership email alone does not identify which prints a person selected later in the store.

When keeping a personal record of multiple purchases, copy the currency along with each amount and record the order date separately from the date on which you downloaded a file. You can retrieve a print on a later visit. A later download date on your device therefore need not match the original order date on the receipt.

Preparing a digital print for personal display

Open the original downloaded file when choosing a display size. A screenshot of the catalog can include the store background, controls or surrounding space. If you create a cropped version to fit a particular screen, save it as a separate file so that you can return to the full composition later. Enlarging a raster image changes how individual pixels appear at close range.

If you arrange to print a file yourself, check the crop, margins and orientation in the print preview. Paper dimensions and the image proportions can differ. Choosing to fill the sheet may trim edges, while fitting the entire composition can leave margins. A print shop handles its own paper selection, production and collection arrangements. Those details are separate from the digital file supplied through the store.

Checking which document you have saved

The attachment is titled “Club Pliego Terms and Conditions.” An artwork file supplied through an order is a PNG image. If you are looking through a downloads folder containing both, open the PDF to read the membership provisions and the corresponding PNG to view the design itself.

If you save a copy of the terms beside an order receipt, use file names that distinguish the date of activation from the date of purchase. A single membership can be associated with several visits and selections. Keeping the dates separate helps you identify the right record when you return to a print later.

A forwarded attachment can be read independently of the membership. Receiving or opening someone else's copy does not activate the club, place a print in a bag or submit an order. Those actions take place through the membership form and the checkout controls on the store.
