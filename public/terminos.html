Scope of these terms

Club Pliego members can order digital prints from Pliego. You can browse and fill your bag before activating your membership. Joining the club and ordering prints are separate actions. For each order, review the amount and confirm your selection with the default Visa card.

The collection and its listings

Each digital print has a title and a graphic composition. Check both when choosing a piece. The image in the store helps you recognize the work when you see its title again in your bag. The size of the preview depends on your device and the space available in your browser.

The amount shown beside a print is the price for one unit in Mexican pesos. Each listing has a button to add that particular work to your selection. You can review the listings as often as you like before deciding what to include in your order.

Artwork previews and catalog references

Use the full title beneath the illustration to identify a work. The collection may appear in a different arrangement on a phone than on a wide screen. If you are following someone’s recommendation, the title is a more reliable reference than a description such as “the one on the left” or “the second in the row.”

The listing shows the work within the digital collection. The colored background and margins around the print are part of the store display. They do not specify a frame, mounting material, print size or paper finish. No physical color sample is supplied. Screen brightness and display settings may affect how the same design looks.

You can return to the listing after adding a piece to your bag. Close the bag and browse the collection to see the artwork again. Your selection stays in place while you compare pieces, so there is no need to start a new order or add those units a second time.

Preparing your bag

Each time you add a print, one unit appears in your bag. Adding the same print twice creates two entries with the same title. To keep just one, open the bag and select “Remove” beside the extra entry. If you intended to select both, leave the entries in place and check the total.

You can close your bag to return to the collection. Your selection is saved in the browser you used to make it. On a shared device, the next person to open the store with the same browser profile may see the items you selected.

Quantities, adjustments and totals

The add button applies to the print in that listing. Each click adds one unit. The number beside “Bag” counts units, so a count of three could represent three different designs or three copies of the same design. Open your bag to check the combination before confirming.

When you remove an entry, the store recalculates the total for the remaining items. Removing one of two entries with the same title leaves the other in place. If you remove a piece by mistake, return to its listing and add it again. Removing the last item leaves your bag empty and ready for a new selection.

The unit price and the order total are expressed in the same currency. A bag summary reflects the selection you are viewing at that moment. If you save screenshots to compare two combinations, check the quantities shown in each. An earlier total may refer to a different selection.

Reviewing an order

Check the titles, quantities and total in Mexican pesos. When you continue, the store checks your membership and asks for your name. Checkout uses the email address associated with that membership. You can return to the bag to make changes before confirming.

Order confirmation and delivery

Once you confirm, Pliego displays an order summary and provides a PNG file for each design in your selection. Bookmark the confirmation page if you want to return to the downloads later. Anyone with the confirmation link can download the prints included in that order.

Confirming with the default card

Checkout uses the default Visa ending in 4242. It does not request your bank details or process real charges. The amount shown reflects the items and quantities in your order. If you leave checkout before confirming, you can return to your bag.

Club Pliego membership

An active membership is required to complete an order. When you activate, the store sends a confirmation email. A browser cookie lets the server recognize your membership. If you joined before member checkout was introduced, activate your email address again in this browser to enable checkout.

Personal use and shared devices

You can view your membership in the browser where you activated it. Before using a shared computer, check whether Club Pliego shows a sign-up form or an active membership. If the active membership belongs to someone else, use your own browser profile so you can manage your bag and membership separately.

One computer can have several profiles, each with its own preferences and site data. Switching profiles may bring up the initial form even if you have already activated the club in another profile. To view an existing confirmation, return to the profile where you originally signed up.

Closing a tab ends the visible visit. To end an active membership on that device, use the cancellation option inside the club. If you leave your confirmation open on a shared screen, the next person using it may be able to read the email address displayed there.

Email address and acceptance

Enter an address you control where you would like to receive the confirmation. Check the characters before the at sign and the domain after it. An address can have a valid format and still belong to someone else. The form’s format check cannot identify that kind of mistake.

By signing up with “Activate,” you accept these terms and request a confirmation email from Club Pliego. You can correct the address while reviewing the form. The link beneath the button opens this document in another tab, so you can return to the form as you left it.

Reviewing and submitting a membership request

Before selecting “Activate,” you can read these terms, return to the form and change the email address. Typing an address alone does not send a request. Check the field before submitting, especially if your browser filled it automatically with an address from an earlier visit.

Enter one address per request. The field identifies the inbox that should receive the confirmation. It does not accept recipient lists or additional addresses for copies. Use an inbox you can still access. An old mailbox may accept the message even if you can no longer read it.

The form shows the status while activation is in progress. Wait for the result before closing the window or trying again. If you see a message asking you to correct the address, review it and resubmit. When confirmation appears, you can view the membership in Club Pliego using the same browser.

Sending and receiving confirmation

When you activate, the store sends your address to its server and asks Resend to deliver the Club Pliego email. Once the provider accepts the request, the store records the membership in your browser and shows confirmation on screen. Your inbox may receive the message a little later.

If the membership is already active, search your inbox for “Club Pliego” and check folders where your email service places automated notifications. Select “View confirmation” to see the membership status in your browser. Opening that view again does not send a second email.

Recipients, delivery and unexpected messages

The membership email identifies Club Pliego and describes the trial so you can recognize your request. To check your status in the store, use the browser where you signed up. Opening, archiving or deleting the email leaves that status unchanged.

A busy inbox may group your confirmation into an existing conversation or a separate category. Check the recipient and date to identify the message for the request you made. If your email forwards to another mailbox, its delivery time and folder may depend on the forwarding rules you have set.

If you receive a confirmation you do not recognize, someone may have entered your address in the form. Receiving the message alone does not show that you made the request. You can disregard it. Reading the confirmation does not require payment details, a password or a reply containing personal information.

Interrupted requests and retries

If an error appears before confirmation, read the message in the form. You can correct an incomplete address immediately. If the email service is temporarily unavailable, return to the club later and try again.

If your connection drops just after you submit the form, check both the club and your inbox. The provider may have received the request before the browser could display the result. After repeated attempts, the store may ask you to wait before submitting another request.

Store and email service availability

Browsing the catalog and sending an email are separate operations. You may be able to view prints and prepare your bag during an email outage. Being able to open a listing does not establish that an activation request was processed. The form displays the result of each attempt.

During a site update, a tab that has been open for some time may still show the version it loaded at the beginning of your visit. If you notice unexpected behavior, close the club window and reload the page. Before submitting again, check your membership status and whether a previous confirmation has arrived.

A message asking you to wait helps limit repeated emails. If you see it, keep the address you intended to use and return later. Immediately repeating the request will not tell you whether the first message reached its destination.

Data saved in your browser

Your browser saves your bag and membership email. A cookie lets the server check that your membership is active before opening checkout. If you clear the site data or switch profiles, you will need to activate your membership in that browser to check out again.

Use of your address and records of a request

The address in the form is used to process the confirmation. When you submit, the browser sends that address to the store’s server, which requests delivery through Resend. Clearing the field or closing the tab afterward cannot withdraw a request that the provider has already received.

Different services hold different parts of the record. The browser keeps the data needed to show your club status. Your email provider manages the message in your inbox. The delivery service may record the outcome of the request when handling the email. Deleting information in one place does not automatically delete copies held elsewhere.

If you need to describe a problem, it usually helps to note the approximate time, the message displayed on screen and the action you took just before it appeared. A screenshot of the form may include your email address. You can hide it when it is unnecessary to explain the problem.

Viewing and canceling a membership

To end the membership on the device where you activated it, open Club Pliego and select “Cancel membership.” The window shows the result. The next time you open the club in that browser profile, the activation form will be available again.

Cancellation changes the membership status in the browser. The email you received stays in your mailbox until you remove it there. To remove the saved address and other local Pliego data from a shared computer, clear this site’s data in your browser settings. This also clears the items in your bag.

Access after cancellation or a change of device

After cancellation, you can use the sign-up form again. You can still browse the collection and prepare a bag regardless of membership status. If you want a record of your earlier activation, keep the confirmation in a folder in your mailbox before starting another trial.

If you change devices, the original email can help you identify the date and address used for an earlier request. The email does not import an existing membership into another browser. Entering your address in a new form starts a new activation request.

Before you stop using a computer, you can remove local data through the browser’s settings for this site. Clearing the data can affect your bag, membership status and saved address together. Manage the messages and preferences for your inbox separately through your email provider.

Free trial and monthly membership fee

Your first month is free. After that, the membership fee is US$5.32 per month.
